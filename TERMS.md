# Terms of use

This is the canonical terms of use for Perqora, also rendered in the running app at `/terms`. If the two ever disagree, this file is the source of truth.

## What Perqora is

Perqora is a free, open-source, community-maintained index of student benefits. It is not a company, it does not process payments, and it is not a party to any transaction between you and a benefit provider.

## Offers change

Every listing shows a verification status and a last-checked date because offers genuinely change, expire, or get restricted without notice. A `VERIFIED` status means the listed information matched the provider's own page as of the date shown, not that it will still be accurate when you visit. Always confirm the current terms on the provider's own site before relying on a benefit, the "Claim benefit" link on every page goes directly there.

## No guarantee of eligibility

Eligibility requirements shown on this site (school type, email domain, country, and similar) are a summary to help you understand whether a benefit is likely to apply to you, not a guarantee. The benefit provider makes the actual eligibility determination, not Perqora.

## Community submissions

Anyone can submit a benefit through `/submit`. A submission is not published automatically and is not itself a claim of accuracy, it enters a review queue and a maintainer verifies it (or doesn't) before any information from it becomes a live listing.
